Backrooms

Level 92233 or as people like to call it "The Last Level" is a level that is said to be the last level of the backrooms. It is said to be an endless loop of stairs that lead up to absolute nothingness. The environment is said to be old and decayed, with the walls being made of a type of ancient stone that is covered in moss and vines. The air is said to be rusty and unhealthy for the lungs. To actually escape this level, you must climb the stairs until you reach the 5000th step of the staircase. Once you reached the 5000th step, you will have to jump off the stairs, which will send you to level 0. One wrong misscount could result to death.

Level 94 is a level that is surrounded by bland green hills with houses on them. Each one of the houses are the same as the other, with the same furniture and the same resources in them. The level is said to be a safe level, with no entities in it. However, when the sun sets, entities will spawn in the houses and they will be hostile. And there will be an even bigger threat waiting outside. What you need to do is to find house 6000 and stay there until the sun rises. Which will result to you being teleported to level "r00000ms".

Level 12 which people call "The Matrix" is a pure white room with a table and a chair in the middle of the room. However, the longer you go, the more the room expands itself as it unlocks new categories like holes or obstables that you have to go through to get to the other side. This level however has no hostile entities and it seems to be a safe level. If you tried to get footage of this level, the video footage would just end up in a white screen or a static screen in gray, black and white. The only way to escape this level is to walk until you find a window on the floor. Once you find the window, open it and beneath it will be pitch black void, jumping into it would result you being in the next level, level 14.
